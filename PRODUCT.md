# Chikitsak: where this could go

## The north star

A family's health memory with a clinician on call. One chat per household that knows every member's history, notices patterns no single visit would, and gets the right human involved at the right time. Most of the time it answers and reassures. Some of the time it hands off to a doctor, and it never misses an emergency.

## What it becomes

- **The household health record that keeps itself.** Every chat, lab report, prescription photo and wearable reading lands on the right person's timeline. "When did Meera last have antibiotics?" has an answer.
- **Longitudinal triage.** "Third fever in two months" or "Dad's fasting sugar is creeping up" are rules over memory, not guesses. The system raises them before anyone asks.
- **Proactive care.** It checks in 12 hours after a fever and reminds you about vaccinations, refills and annual checkups for each member. Pi Durable's scheduled tasks make this cheap: the agent sleeps for free until it is due.
- **A doctor-in-the-loop marketplace.** DOCTOR handoffs go to a queue with a ready-made case summary (history, facts, red-flag screen), so a 3-minute review replaces a 15-minute consultation. Doctors' decisions flow back into the episode.
- **Caregiver mode.** Adult children managing an ageing parent's health from another city share one household with roles and permissions.
- **Voice and WhatsApp first** for India, in local languages, because that is where families already talk.

## Where it could be used

- Families, directly, as a consumer app.
- Insurers and employers, as a triage front door that cuts unnecessary ER and OPD visits.
- Clinics and hospital chains, as an after-hours assistant that routes to their own doctors.
- Pharmacies, for refill and interaction checks against the household's medicine list.
- Elder-care services, for daily check-ins with escalation.

## What stays true

The model talks; code decides. Every new capability is a plug-in that can add facts and rules but can only make the system more cautious. That is what lets a clinical team sign off on it.
